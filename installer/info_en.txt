HOW TO USE
1. Start GIMP 3.
2. Open Filters > Photoshop > Manage plug-ins...
3. Click "Add directory..." and choose a folder with your Photoshop plug-ins (.8bf files).
   PSHost tests every plug-in and tells you whether it will be used and why not.
4. Accept the restart of GIMP when asked. The filters then appear in Filters > Photoshop > <Category>.

IMPORTANT
- This installer does NOT install any Photoshop plug-ins. You add your own folders later.
- Only 64-bit Photoshop FILTER plug-ins (.8bf) are supported. 32-bit plug-ins are rejected.
- Adding a plug-in runs third-party code during the test and when you use the filter. Add only plug-ins from sources you trust.
- A plug-in that crashes or hangs runs in a separate process and does not close GIMP.
- This installer is not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning ("Windows protected your PC").
  Choose "More info" > "Run anyway" only if you obtained the installer from a source you trust.
